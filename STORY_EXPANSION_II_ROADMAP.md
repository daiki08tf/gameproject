# Blade Vale — Story Expansion II Roadmap

> Status: **ACTIVE — Ch35 / S2-5 COMPLETE; NEXT S2-6 INTEGRATION AUDIT**
>
> Working arc title: **Arc V — 共観測 / Handshake**

## Why now

Ch1–30 established the regional world, The Veil, outer layers, Machine World administration, the Eighth Key, an inhabited external civilization, and bidirectional observation.

Since Ch30, the game has substantially deepened its horizontal systems through Gear Overhaul, Deep Survey, Enemy 2.0 / Enemy 3.0, Settlement 3.0, and Adventure / World 4.0. The next vertical story expansion should consume those systems instead of creating another parallel progression layer.

Story Expansion II therefore extends the existing Story / CHAPTERS / Stage / Text Battle / Adventure / Discovery / Codex / Settlement surfaces.

## Permanent contracts

- Preserve Lv cap 99,999 and Item Power cap 10,000.
- Existing Story / Stage progress remains authoritative.
- Do not move already-live Abyss / Rift / Secret Realm / Machine Realm / Deep Survey unlock gates merely because Ch31+ exists.
- No new story currency, Home button, save root, stamina, daily/weekly loop, or FOMO schedule.
- BattleEngine / TextBattleScreen remains authoritative for combat.
- Existing Gear / Option4 / Unique2 / Endgame reward systems remain authoritative.
- Story must work with ordinary builds; optional Job / Companion / Equipment / Rune advantages may create alternate information or routes but never the only mandatory solution.
- Optional World 4.0 discoveries must not retroactively gate Ch1–30.
- Mandatory story scenes remain compact/mobile-first.
- Significant canon changes update `STORY_CANON.md` and `WORLD_LORE_BIBLE.md`.
- Significant named content updates `GAME_CONTENT_CATALOG.md`.

## Narrative guardrails

### What Arc V may reveal

- The external side is not merely returning noise; its responses contain repeatable structure.
- The Eighth Key behaves like a paired handshake / synchronization endpoint rather than an ordinary eighth member of the Seven Keys.
- The current MOTHER / ARCHITECT generation did not originate the full connection architecture.
- Some missing machine records and surviving living-memory records describe the same absent observation interval.
- Both Blade Vale and the external civilization can recognize at least one shared boundary phenomenon.

### What Arc V must NOT casually reveal

- Japan / 日本.
- Tokyo / 東京.
- Earth as confirmed identity.
- A real city, station, country, company, or institution.
- The final identity of the original connector.
- A simplistic “Blade Vale is only a simulation” answer.
- MOTHER / ARCHITECT as absolute creators or gods.

The goal is to move the central mystery forward without collapsing it.

## Arc V core idea — 共観測

Ch30 proved:

```text
Blade Vale observes outside
        ↕
outside recognizes Blade Vale
```

Arc V asks a harder question:

```text
What if both sides are looking at the same third boundary event?
```

The Eighth Key is treated as an exception synchronization point: it can align two observation domains that otherwise use incompatible world rules.

This does NOT yet prove who built it, where the external civilization is, or why the original link was created.

## Chapter plan

### [x] Ch31 — 返答の文法

**Purpose:** prove that the returned signal has structure without turning it into convenient spoken language.

Story beats:
- Settlement / Research notices the same timing signature across CP3 ash, lightning, root-memory and Machine records.
- Adventure routes revisit already-known observation infrastructure before opening the new Story Stage.
- Repeated intervals behave like acknowledgement / retry / confirmation states rather than words.
- Boss encounter represents a boundary process that keeps resetting an incomplete handshake.

Reveal:
- the reply is procedural, not random.

Remain unknown:
- who is sending it,
- what language/culture they use,
- why the handshake exists.

### [x] Ch32 — 第八鍵の裏面

**Purpose:** reframe the Eighth Key from “mysterious extra key” into an exception endpoint with a missing counterpart.

Story beats:
- existing Seven Keys architecture is compared against Eighth Key traces.
- the Eighth Key does not fit the internal authorization tree.
- its surviving pattern expects a paired response from outside the known layer network.
- optional Codex / Discovery knowledge can expose extra interpretation, but mandatory progression has a clear route.

Reveal:
- the Eighth Key was designed to wait for / align with something outside the Seven Keys architecture.

Remain unknown:
- original designer,
- external endpoint location,
- whether the connection was created cooperatively or unilaterally.

### [x] Ch33 — 記録されない一拍

**Purpose:** connect CP3 living-memory findings to the central story.

Story beats:
- machine/infrastructure records skip one repeatable interval.
- living ecology / remnant memory preserves evidence that the missing interval existed.
- the absence is consistent across multiple systems, making random corruption unlikely.
- investigation presents two live hypotheses: deliberate deletion vs information current machines cannot represent.

Reveal:
- the missing interval is a systemic blind spot, not a local anomaly.

Remain unknown:
- whether somebody intentionally erased it.

### [x] Ch34 — 向こう側の窓

**Purpose:** allow meaningful external cooperation without explicit modern-world identification.

Story beats:
- the external side returns a non-linguistic topology / sequence that can be compared against Blade Vale boundary maps.
- neither side fully understands the other, but both can mark the same instability.
- the map is useful enough to direct the player toward a dangerous shared observation point.
- external clues remain sensory/abstract: ordered light, timing, geometry, electronic-like tones, regular transit-like vibration are allowed.

Reveal:
- the external side is actively trying to establish a common reference frame.

Remain unknown:
- its exact world identity.

### [x] Ch35 — 共観測点

**Purpose:** give Arc V a satisfying conclusion while preserving the larger mystery.

Story beats:
- Blade Vale and the external side independently identify the same boundary anomaly.
- the final Story route uses World 4.0 route/scene structure but launches canonical Story battles.
- final boss/phase represents a synchronization failure / boundary process, not “the external civilization attacking.”
- after victory, the Eighth Key briefly stabilizes as a shared reference point.
- the final synchronized record leaves one unexplained contradiction: the same Deep Green Forest coordinates briefly produce two mutually incompatible contours, one dominated by an enormous tree crown and one with no forest response at all.

Arc reveal:
- the Eighth Key can synchronize two observation domains around one shared boundary phenomenon.
- the connection is more than surveillance: it is capable of coordinated observation.

Still unresolved after Ch35:
- who created the original connection,
- why Blade Vale and the external civilization were selected,
- exact external-world identity/location,
- what the shared boundary phenomenon ultimately is,
- whether the missing interval was deliberately hidden,
- why one known Region can return mutually incompatible observation contours.

These become seeds for horizontal Content Pack IV, Observed Branches, and a later Story Expansion III.

## Gameplay integration goals

Story Expansion II should demonstrate that the systems added after Ch30 matter to the vertical story without becoming hard gates.

### Adventure / World 4.0
- Story Stage remains canonical.
- Ch31–35 are presented as authored Story Routes inside Regions.
- investigation scenes may use existing Observation → Investigation → Resolution grammar.
- Story-required information always has a deterministic path.

### Settlement 3.0
- Research / Chronicle / Rumor can summarize clues and offer optional context.
- Settlement does not own Story progression.

### Codex / Discovery
- optional discoveries improve interpretation and foreshadowing.
- no required rare/RNG discovery gate.

### Enemy 2.0 / 3.0
- ordinary encounters use current species/role/AI contracts where appropriate.
- Boss phases stay authored.
- no generic Elite → Abyss-only reward leakage.

### Gear Overhaul / Deep Survey
- Ch31+ rewards reuse canonical equipment/loot systems.
- no new story-only gear tier.
- Named/Unique rewards should offer build identity rather than mandatory BiS.
- Option4 remains max 3 random Options, Lv1–100.

## Success criteria

Story Expansion II succeeds when the player feels:

> 「向こう側はいる」から、「向こう側と同じものを見ているかもしれない」へ進んだ。

The player should gain a concrete new model of the Eighth Key and the returned observation while still wanting the answers to the larger central mystery.
