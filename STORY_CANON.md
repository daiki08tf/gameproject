# Blade Vale — Story Canon

> High-level narrative guardrails. `WORLD_LORE_BIBLE.md` holds the detailed human-readable setting; gameplay code remains authoritative for exact progression, levels and rewards.

## Central mystery

**なぜBlade Valeと外側の文明は接続されているのか。誰が接続し、何を観測しているのか。**

The journey begins as a regional fantasy adventure and gradually becomes an investigation into world boundaries, maintenance systems and external observation. The answer remains intentionally incomplete after Ch30.

## Canon world layers

- **人界 / Human Realm** — protagonist's home layer.
- **天界 / Heaven** — powerful neighboring layer, not the absolute creator.
- **冥界 / Underworld** — memory/death/remnant layer, not merely an afterlife.
- **境界層 / Boundary Layer** — routes, seals, gates, observation stations and isolation sectors between worlds.
- **機界 / Machine World** — artificial administration / repair / observation layer.
- **外側の文明** — inhabited, highly ordered civilization beyond the known layers; exact location and identity remain withheld.

## The Veil

**The Veil is a network, not one wall.** It separates, connects, seals and observes world layers.

The shutdown of Black Iron Machine Castle destabilized already weakened maintenance structures and exposed routes that had been hidden or sealed.

Some enemies were guardians rather than villains. 原初の獣アビス is the clearest example: it was holding a fracture closed from inside.

## Seven Keys / Eighth Key

The Seven Keys belong to the known internal control architecture.

The Eighth Key is not simply an eighth member. Ch26–30 establish that it behaves like an exception connection inserted outside the accepted seven-key management path. Its origin and purpose remain unresolved.

## MOTHER / ARCHITECT

- MOTHER = operational administration system.
- ARCHITECT = design / repair system.
- Neither is the creator of the entire world network.
- Ch28 confirms Machine World itself was subject to external audit / observation.

Never casually rewrite administrator-level entities as absolute gods or creators.

## Story arcs

### Arc I — Ch1–15: 地上の旅
Grounded RPG adventure with recurring impossible symbols, ruins and machinery.

**Reveal:** the world has seams.

### Arc II — Ch16–20: The Veil
The boundary becomes explicit. Ch19 names The Veil; Ch20 reveals the guardian truth.

**Reveal:** the boundary is failing.

### Arc III — Ch21–25: 外縁世界
Outer layers and the Boundary Throne show Blade Vale is one node in a larger system.

**Reveal:** the world is part of a network.

### Arc IV — Ch26–30: 逆観測 / 外部観測
- Ch26: Eighth Key becomes an exception connection.
- Ch27: ordered lights, metallic vibration and luminous surfaces imply an inhabited external civilization.
- Ch28: MOTHER / ARCHITECT are administrators under observation.
- Ch29: observation becomes bidirectional and the external side responds.
- Ch30: external civilization and bidirectional recognition are confirmed, but place name, reason for connection and original connector remain unknown.

**Reveal:** the connection is real and the other side can answer.

### Horizontal continuation — Content Pack III
Returned observation begins altering already-explored regions. The first anomalies are transformed local ecology, infrastructure and memory rather than simple alien invasions.

## Clue ladder

1. **違和感** — repeated symbols, impossible materials, inconsistent histories.
2. **境界の兆候** — spatial distortion and layer interference.
3. **The Veil** — keys, fractures, guardian truth.
4. **境界網** — outer worlds, Boundary Throne, Eighth Key.
5. **観測者** — machine audits, external observation and selection language.
6. **外側の文明** — regular lights, rail-like sound, electronic tones, dates/devices/writing-like traces.
7. **双方向観測** — the external side recognizes and answers contact.

## Modern-world restraint

Sensory clues are allowed. Explicit identification is not yet canon.

Do not casually reveal:
- Japan / 日本
- Tokyo / 東京
- Earth as a confirmed identity
- a real station, city or country

Those require a deliberate future story reveal.

## Writing rules

- Story supports exploration and gameplay; do not turn the game into a separate visual novel.
- Mandatory text stays compact for mobile.
- Reveal one useful clue at a time.
- Give existing mechanics narrative meaning before inventing lore-only systems.
- Old content should gain new interpretations rather than being retconned away.
- Preserve meaningful unknown / `?` states.
- Narrative code must not silently alter level curves, rewards or established endgame gates.
- Optional horizontal discoveries must not retroactively gate Ch1–30.
- Boss dialogue and discoveries should reinforce actual mechanics where possible.
