# Enemy 3.0 B7 — Boss Phase AI 2.0

B7 preserves the existing authored Boss AI contract and only deepens Phase 2 pressure.

- Existing 50% HP phase transition remains authoritative.
- Existing telegraph kinds, special attack damage multipliers and summon behavior remain unchanged.
- Phase 2 gains +8% effective ATK and +8% effective SPD.
- Every second actionable Phase 2 turn, existing special/summon countdowns above 1 are shortened by one extra turn.
- Active telegraphs are never skipped, replaced or accelerated.
- No new reward, save, status, Elite, Rare or World Tier semantics are introduced.
