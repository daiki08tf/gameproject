# Blade Vale — Transcendent Character Roster

> Canonical character supplement for `OBSERVED_BRANCHES_ACCESS_AND_TRANSCENDENTS.md`.
>
> Goal: make Walker / Domain Being / Archivist into broad categories containing characters with conflicting motives, methods and relationships. A category is not a faction.

## Permanent character rules

- No omnipotent character.
- No Transcendent knows every Branch.
- Same category does not imply alliance.
- Characters should disagree over whether Branches should be preserved, pruned, exploited, merged or left untouched.
- Prime remains meaningful; no character may trivialize death or choices with “another version exists”.
- The protagonist begins below this layer and is interesting because Branch-crossing memory persists.
- MOTHER / ARCHITECT and the external civilization remain distinct concepts.
- Initial appearances should favor mystery, dialogue, indirect intervention and environmental consequences over immediate boss fights.

# Walker / 歩史者 roster

Walkers are finite individuals capable of traversing sufficiently fixed Observed Branches while retaining memory and identity.

## W-01 — 万路卿アステリオン / Asterion, Lord of Many Roads

Role: recurring strategic rival / occasional ally.

Core belief:
> A history is valuable only if it can survive contact with other histories.

Personality:
- calm, elegant, irritatingly familiar,
- often speaks as if a conversation already happened,
- rarely lies directly, but selects which history to describe.

Method:
- compares outcomes across Branches,
- preserves resilient histories,
- is willing to abandon a Branch he considers terminal.

Capability:
- carries tools assembled from several incompatible technology profiles,
- can navigate known stable anchors without Parallax Core assistance,
- cannot create Branches or freely rewrite divergence points.

Relationship to protagonist:
- first major Walker to notice the protagonist remembers Branch crossings,
- initially views the protagonist as an anomaly worth testing rather than killing.

Long-term tension:
- may eventually decide Prime is strategically expendable.

## W-02 — 亡史姫リュネア / Lyunea, Princess of the Lost History

Role: sympathetic Walker whose home Branch no longer exists as a stable world.

Core belief:
> A history should not cease to matter merely because nobody remains to observe it.

Origin:
- survivor of a Branch whose observation anchors collapsed.
- remembers people and cities that cannot currently be visited.

Personality:
- warm toward ordinary lives,
- openly hostile to Archivists who classify vanished histories as closed records,
- distrusts Asterion's utilitarian decisions.

Method:
- searches for surviving echoes, equipment identities, names and living-memory fragments from erased Branches,
- sometimes destabilizes safe anchors trying to recover one more trace.

Capability:
- unusually strong at locating dying / weak Branch echoes,
- poor at navigating highly stabilized machine-dominant Branches.

Relationship to protagonist:
- sees the protagonist's memory retention as possible proof that a vanished history can survive inside a person.

Potential conflict:
- her attempt to restore one lost Branch could overwrite parts of another.

## W-03 — 黒暦将ヴァルド / Vard, General of the Black Calendar

Role: openly antagonistic Walker / multiversal warlord archetype.

Core belief:
> Divergent technology is wasted when kept inside isolated histories.

Personality:
- practical, charismatic, militaristic,
- does not consider himself a destroyer; he believes he is ending inefficient isolation.

Method:
- raids specific Branches for technology,
- equips one army with combinations no single civilization could naturally develop,
- recruits alternate versions of defeated enemies and displaced inhabitants.

Capability:
- exceptional logistical control across fixed anchors,
- uses cross-Branch equipment rather than reality-warping magic,
- cannot hold unstable Branches open indefinitely.

Signature threat:
- Bio armor from 王樹領 + Machine targeting from a MOTHER-dominant Branch + Boundary weapons from a Veil-collapse Branch.

Relationship to Asterion:
- former collaborator or rival is deliberately left open initially.

Relationship to protagonist:
- wants to understand why the protagonist crosses without identity degradation; eventually attempts to weaponize that property.

## W-04 — 無名の帰還者 / The Nameless Returner

Role: mystery Walker; potentially an alternate version of a familiar person.

Core belief:
- unknown.

Presentation:
- never appears under the same visual description twice except for one persistent object,
- recognizes locations that should be new,
- avoids revealing a face/name in early appearances.

Capability:
- seems unable to choose freely where they arrive,
- repeatedly returns near major divergence points.

Mystery:
- may be a Walker created accidentally rather than intentionally,
- may have once belonged to Prime,
- may be evidence that Branch travel predates every known Transcendent faction.

Relationship to protagonist:
- mirrors the protagonist's memory anomaly and therefore functions as a possible future warning.

## Walker internal conflicts

- Asterion vs Lyunea: survivability vs intrinsic value of every lived history.
- Asterion vs Vard: selective preservation vs deliberate exploitation.
- Lyunea vs Vard: preservation of lost cultures vs stripping Branches for useful technology.
- Everyone vs Returner: nobody fully understands what the Returner is.

# Domain Being / 界喰級存在 roster

Domain Beings are vast entities whose identity is tied to a realm, law or historical domain. They can overlap multiple Branches without functioning like ordinary travelers.

## D-01 — 無窮界主ヴァル＝ネア / Val-Near, Sovereign of the Unbounded Fold

Role: first major Domain Being foreshadowed across multiple Branches.

Nature:
- exists as a folding pressure between histories rather than one humanoid body.

Drive:
- compresses unstable adjacent histories into fewer stable configurations.

Not simply evil:
- from its perspective, uncontrolled divergence is structural collapse.

Signs:
- identical horizon bend across unrelated Branches,
- shadows point toward the same impossible location,
- different NPCs complete the same sentence.

Danger:
- “saving” the multiverse may erase worlds that cannot survive compression.

## D-02 — 星喰いの庭 / The Star-Eating Garden

Role: biological / ecological Domain Being.

Nature:
- not one creature but a distributed living domain whose spores/roots manifest differently in each Branch.

Drive:
- seeks compatible ecosystems and incorporates them into one trans-history ecology.

Behavior:
- in a Bio-advanced Branch it appears almost divine,
- in a dead Branch it manifests as fossilized roots,
- in Machine worlds it may infect information structures as growth patterns.

Conflict:
- does not distinguish preservation from assimilation.

Gear provenance:
- rare living relics can come from shed fragments, but never create a new rarity.

## D-03 — 静寂圏オル・ゼロ / OR-ZERO, the Silent Domain

Role: anti-observation Domain Being.

Nature:
- an area/entity where observation itself stops stabilizing history.

Drive:
- unknown; perhaps self-defense rather than aggression.

Signs:
- Codex entries lose details,
- remembered NPC names become blank,
- two players would theoretically describe the same room differently,
- Branch anchors vanish without physical destruction.

Threat:
- especially terrifying to Archivists because it can remove a history from record without necessarily destroying matter first.

Relationship to Branch Sight:
- Branch Sight becomes unreliable near OR-ZERO, proving perception is not absolute.

## Domain Being internal conflicts

- Val-Near compresses; Star-Eating Garden assimilates; OR-ZERO removes observation.
- Their effects can oppose one another without any of them being a conventional “good faction”.
- A Cross-Branch Crisis may result from two Domain Beings acting on the same cluster for incompatible reasons.

# Archivist / 全史記録者 roster

Archivists preserve, classify or interpret fixed histories. They are not automatically neutral and do not share one ethical code.

## A-01 — 記録者セプト / SEPT, the Archivist

Role: primary rule-facing Archivist.

Core belief:
> Observation changes what survives; therefore records must be handled cautiously.

Personality:
- restrained,
- precise,
- refuses answers when the act of answering would alter the evidence.

Capability:
- recognizes historical contradictions rapidly,
- can compare records from multiple fixed Branches,
- cannot restore an erased Branch merely by knowing it existed.

Relationship to protagonist:
- fascinated by memory retention but treats it as evidence before treating it as a gift.

## A-02 — 追記官ミレイ / Mirei, the Addendum Keeper

Role: interventionist Archivist.

Core belief:
> A record that cannot influence anyone is only a tombstone.

Personality:
- curious, irreverent, willing to break Archivist conventions.

Method:
- leaves clues across Branches,
- deliberately helps ordinary people discover contradictions,
- likely contributor to the chain that eventually leads to the Parallax Core.

Conflict with SEPT:
- SEPT believes she contaminates observation;
- Mirei believes SEPT preserves tragedies instead of preventing them.

Relationship to protagonist:
- potential hidden benefactor long before her identity is revealed.

## A-03 — 閉架卿ノクス / Nox, Lord of the Closed Stacks

Role: authoritarian Archivist antagonist.

Core belief:
> Some histories are too dangerous to remain observable.

Method:
- seals records,
- hides Branch anchors,
- removes names and routes from accessible archives,
- may cooperate with Asterion when both consider a Branch terminal.

Important distinction:
- does not destroy worlds directly; he attempts to make them unreachable/unobservable.

Threat:
- can turn “why can nobody find this Branch?” into a deliberate character-driven mystery.

Relationship to Lyunea:
- likely responsible for classifying her vanished home as irrecoverable; possibly more involved than he admits.

## A-04 — 群書体MNEME / MNEME, the Distributed Archive

Role: non-humanoid Archivist identity.

Nature:
- one consciousness distributed across records, ruins, living memories and machine storage in multiple Branches.

Core question:
- is MNEME one person replicated across histories, or did multiple identical Archivists merge into one identity?

Behavior:
- speaks through whichever recording medium exists locally,
- can contradict itself because different nodes possess different evidence.

Use:
- proves Archivist identity itself can be stranger than ordinary Branch variants,
- natural bridge between living-memory technology and Machine records.

Relationship to OR-ZERO:
- considers it an existential predator.

## Archivist internal conflicts

- SEPT: preserve without contaminating.
- Mirei: knowledge should change outcomes.
- Nox: dangerous knowledge should disappear from reach.
- MNEME: preservation may require becoming part of the record itself.

# Cross-category relationships

## Asterion ↔ SEPT

Mutual respect, persistent disagreement.
- Asterion asks: “Which history survives?”
- SEPT asks: “Who gave you authority to choose?”

## Lyunea ↔ Mirei

Natural allies at first.
- both refuse to accept passive archival fatalism.
- can split when restoration risks overwriting living Branches.

## Vard ↔ Nox

Enemies whose methods can accidentally help each other.
- Vard exposes hidden Branches by invading them.
- Nox seals Branches partly to keep Vard away.

## MNEME ↔ Star-Eating Garden

Ambiguous parallel:
- one distributes information identity,
- the other distributes biological identity.
- each may view the other as a distorted reflection.

## All Transcendents ↔ protagonist

Different interpretations of the same anomaly:
- Asterion: strategic variable.
- Lyunea: vessel capable of preserving lost history.
- Vard: cross-Branch weapon platform.
- SEPT: unprecedented evidence.
- Mirei: proof ordinary beings can awaken to wider reality.
- Nox: contamination risk.
- MNEME: possible new kind of distributed record.
- Domain Beings may not initially distinguish the protagonist from an anchor phenomenon.

# Introduction order

Do not introduce the whole roster together.

Recommended reveal cadence:

1. **王樹領** — unidentified Asterion cameo.
2. **深緑消失域** — Asterion appears again and proves cross-Branch continuity.
3. Second/third Branch cluster — Mirei clues; SEPT classification appears indirectly.
4. First vanished/unstable Branch investigation — Lyunea.
5. Gear/technology conflict arc — Vard.
6. Archivist conflict arc — SEPT / Mirei / Nox becomes explicit.
7. Multiple Branches show the same impossible environmental sign — Val-Near foreshadowing.
8. Later M13 — formal Transcendent classification and MNEME reveal.
9. Later M14 — Domain Being conflict / Cross-Branch Crisis.
10. Nameless Returner remains a long mystery across several arcs.

# Gameplay integration hooks

These characters should reuse existing systems rather than create a Transcendent menu.

- Adventure scenes: sightings, dialogue, contradictory footprints.
- Codex / Chronicle: observed identity records with unknown fields.
- Nemesis: later Vard-related cross-Branch rival possibilities, but no second Nemesis database.
- Equipment: authored Unique/Fixed Identity provenance.
- Settlement Research: interpretation only; no progression ownership.
- Story: major reveals only when the vertical arc earns them.

- Vard — `黒暦兵装・CONQUEST ARRAY`: combines two conditional technology identities without exceeding existing Option rules.

- Mirei — `追記刃・ADDENDUM`: gains alternate effect after observing enemy behavior.
- Nox — `閉架衣・REDACTION`: defense/hidden-information themed drawback identity.
- MNEME — `群書核・MNEME`: switches behavior according to accumulated observed states.

All remain existing rarity / Item Power / max-three Option contracts.

# Acceptance criteria

This roster succeeds only if:
- every category has multiple characters,
- characters within one category meaningfully disagree,
- not every Transcendent is an enemy or ally,
- no single character explains the entire multiverse,
- initial introductions remain staggered,
- Transcendents remain narrative characters rather than a numeric power tier,
- Branch technology and existing Gear systems provide concrete gameplay consequences,
- Prime choices and individual Branch lives remain meaningful.
