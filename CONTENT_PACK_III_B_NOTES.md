1. **返信印の正体**

2. **返送周期の一致**
   - 第九照準廊 → 異記憶根室 → ACK-WARDEN
   - conclusion: thunder, roots and ash are different local translations of one returned observation pulse.

3. **生きた記憶だけが残したもの**
   - 異記憶根室 → RETURN-CLOCK → CINDER-REPLY
   - conclusion: living ecology retains an observation interval missing from machine/infrastructure records.
   - intentionally unresolved: deliberate deletion vs information that machines cannot record.
